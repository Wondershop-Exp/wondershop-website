What To Expect

Watch science come alive in the most exciting way! Our interactive Science Show is packed with fascinating experiments involving fire, smoke, colour-changing reactions, and other incredible demonstrations that spark curiosity and wonder. Children don't just watch—they become part of the action! Safe, engaging, and full of surprises, it's the perfect blend of entertainment and hands-on discovery for curious young minds.
