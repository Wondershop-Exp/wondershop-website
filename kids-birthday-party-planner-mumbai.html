Questions parents ask

How much does a kids birthday party planner cost in Mumbai?

Our themed packages start at ₹22,500 (Unicorn Magic, up to 25 kids) and ₹25,000 (Spy Adventure). The final price depends on the number of kids, venue, décor size and add-ons like shows, DJ or return gifts. The party builder shows the exact price as you choose.

Do you plan birthday parties at home?

Yes. Many of our parties are in flats and society clubhouses. We plan décor and activities to fit the space, and handle setup and clean-up.

How early should I book?

Two to three weeks ahead is ideal, and earlier for weekends during school holidays. If your date is sooner, WhatsApp us and we'll tell you what's possible.

What age groups do you plan for?

Everything from first birthdays and toddler parties to tweens. Activities and the host's games are chosen for your child's age, with options for mixed-age groups.

Can I plan a party on a low budget?

Yes. Pick the essentials (décor plus a host, or décor plus one activity) in the builder, or tell us your budget on WhatsApp and we'll suggest the best combination.

Do you do baby showers and other celebrations?

Yes. Alongside kids' birthdays we plan baby showers, first-birthday milestones and family celebrations.
