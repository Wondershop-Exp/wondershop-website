What's included

Decor
Premium is included by default — a 1-panel balloon arch (up to 300 balloons, up to 3 colours) with a flex print or LED Happy Birthday + name and an age foil balloon. Switch to a different tier below if you'd like.
  • 1-panel balloon arch
  • Up to 300 balloons (up to 3 colours)
  • Flex print or LED Happy Birthday + name
  • Age foil balloon
  • Cutouts as per reference photo
  • Welcome decor (add-on)
  • Cake table setup (add-on)
Engagement
90 minutes of turf games is included by default — host, props and trainers, scaled to your headcount. Upgrade to 120 minutes below.
  • Trained host, props & trainers
  • Games drawn from: Clap Lap, Snap, Pyramid, Throw, Footie Obstacle Race, Dizzy Football, One-Legged Dog & the Bone, Introduction to Gymnastics, Relay Race, Team Games, Tug of War
  • Scaled to your number of kids
  • Mic + speaker included
Music
Music Essential included as standard — 1 speaker, 1 mixer, 2 mics, 1 operator, suitable for gatherings up to 50 people. Upgrade to Music Plus below.
E-Invite
Custom digital invite in the turf theme with your child's name, date, venue and RSVP details. Shareable on WhatsApp.
  • Turf-themed design
  • Child's name & age personalized
  • Date, time & venue details
  • RSVP number included
  • Shareable via WhatsApp & email
  • Delivered 5 days before the event
