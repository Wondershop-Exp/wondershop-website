Teams

Teams and audio are remembered on this tablet for each booking ID. Audio: upload 1–2 recordings per team (mp3, m4a, wav). One is picked at random on each sorting. If none is uploaded, the hat announces the team in a spoken voice.

The Hat

Use the drawn hat, or upload a photo of your own Sorting Hat prop. A cut-out PNG with a transparent background looks best. It's saved on this tablet for every party.

Laptop: download a copy and double-click it to open in Chrome or Safari, no internet needed.
