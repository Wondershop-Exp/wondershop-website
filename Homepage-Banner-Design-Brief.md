# Wondershop Experiences — Homepage Banner Design Brief

**Purpose:** 7–8 new hero banner images for the homepage rotating carousel (top-right of the homepage, the large rounded image box next to the main headline).

## 1. What these banners are for

The homepage hero has a large image box that auto-rotates through several photos every ~5 seconds, with a small caption badge in the bottom-left corner (e.g. "Live Event · Mumbai"). It's the first visual thing a visitor sees, before they've read a word of copy — so its job is to instantly say *"real kids, real parties, real quality"* and make a parent feel: *this is who I want planning my child's birthday.*

Right now this rotation leans on raw event photography. We want 7–8 **purpose-shot or purpose-composed banners** that are more consistent, more polished, and each one deliberately built around one of our core services — while still feeling like a real moment from a real party, not a stock photo or an illustration.

## 2. The non-negotiable: realistic, not illustrated

- Photographic, not illustrated/cartoon/clipart style.
- Should look like it was shot at an actual premium birthday event — natural light or warm event lighting, real decor, real kids' genuine expressions (mid-laugh, mid-surprise, mid-focus) rather than posed stock-photo smiles.
- Avoid anything that reads as generic stock photography or obviously AI-generated. If AI tools are used in production, the output needs enough retouching/compositing polish that it's indistinguishable from real event photography.
- Every image should carry all three of: **a specific service**, **production quality** (the decor/setup should look genuinely premium, not thrown together), and **customer emotion** (a child's or parent's authentic reaction — wonder, joy, focus, laughter). A technically nice photo of an empty decorated room isn't enough on its own; there should be a person feeling something in it.

## 3. The 8 banners

One per core service, each framed around a specific emotional beat:

1. **Themed Decor Reveal** — A fully dressed venue (balloon arch, backdrop, themed props) at the moment the birthday child first sees it. Capture the "walking in" reaction — wide eyes, hands up, big smile — with the decor clearly in frame behind/around them.
2. **Activities in Motion** — Kids genuinely absorbed in a hands-on activity (a science show, treasure hunt, craft station, sports activity). Mid-action, not posed — focus, concentration, or a burst of laughter.
3. **Host Energy** — Our host/entertainer mid-performance with a mic, surrounded by a crowd of engaged kids reaching up or cheering. This one should feel loud and alive.
4. **DJ & Dance Floor** — Kids dancing under party lighting, DJ visible softly in the background. A little motion blur is welcome here — it should feel like movement and music, not a still pose.
5. **Piñata Moment** — The split-second a piñata bursts and candy showers down — kids' delighted, startled-happy reactions are the whole point of this shot.
6. **E-Invite / Anticipation** — A quieter, warmer moment: a parent and child together looking at a phone or tablet, smiling at a digital invite design. This represents the planning stage before the party — softer lighting, more intimate than the action shots.
7. **Photographer / Candid Memories** — A candid family moment being captured — a parent laughing with their child — with a photographer and camera visible in soft foreground blur. This one says "we capture the memories, you don't have to."
8. **Return Gifts / Thank-You Moment** — Kids receiving or opening their return-gift bags, or a close-up of a beautifully packaged gift bag with happy kids softly out of focus behind it. The goodbye-that-feels-good moment.

*(Optional 9th, if you'd like to swap one out or add one: a mascot/character appearance moment — a costumed mascot interacting with delighted kids.)*

## 4. Technical specs

| | |
|---|---|
| **Aspect ratio** | 1:1 (square) |
| **Delivered size** | 1600 × 1600px minimum (2400 × 2400px preferred, for retina/future use) |
| **Format** | JPG, sRGB, high quality (minimal compression artifacts) |
| **File naming** | Descriptive slug, e.g. `banner-decor-reveal.jpg`, `banner-host-energy.jpg` |

**Crop safety:** The image is displayed with `object-fit: cover` and is anchored to the **top** of the frame (`object-position: center top`). This means:
- On some screen widths the box gets shorter/wider, and the **bottom** of the image is what gets trimmed first — so keep faces and key subject matter in the **upper two-thirds** of the frame.
- Keep the outer ~10% border on all sides free of critical detail (text, faces right at the edge) since side-cropping also happens on very narrow or very wide screens.
- A small caption badge (white text on a dark rounded pill, ~16px from the edge) sits in the **bottom-left corner** — the image doesn't need to accommodate text, but avoid having your single most important visual detail exactly in that corner.

## 5. Look & feel

- **Color grading:** warm, bright, soft — not moody or dark. A light, consistent grade across all 8 so the rotation feels like one cohesive set rather than 8 random photos.
- **Brand palette nod:** our brand colors are a soft lilac (#B59DDE), pink (#E65A96), and cream/gold accents — doesn't need to be forced into the color grade, but is a nice-to-have if it shows up naturally in balloon colors, decor, or props.
- **Diversity:** vary children's ages (toddler through pre-teen), gender, and setting (indoor home, outdoor, banquet hall) across the 8 so the set feels broadly representative, not repetitive.

Happy to share a folder of our existing real event photography for tone/quality reference on request — the goal is for these 8 to feel like the *best, most consistent* version of what's already being captured at our events, not a stylistic departure from it.
